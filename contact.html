About Me

LK

Liam Kidd

Auckland-based, born and raised in New Zealand. European background but my real food culture is everything I picked up growing up here — from dairies and school canteens to my mates' family dinners.

Outside of food I play volleyball, go to the gym, and read manhua. I like travelling and trying new things, and food is usually part of that.

This site is about the food that actually shaped me — not where I came from on paper, but what I grew up eating.
